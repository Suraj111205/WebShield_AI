Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use WebShield AI, we collect the following information:

  • Website URLs you submit for scanning. We use these solely to perform the security scan you requested.
  • Account information (email address and name) if you choose to create an account.
  • IP address and basic request metadata for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store passwords of websites you scan.
  • We do not install tracking cookies for advertising purposes.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To perform security scans on the URLs you provide.
  • To generate and deliver PDF security reports.
  • To manage your account and scan history if you register.
  • To enforce scan limits for unauthenticated users.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Scan results and generated reports are stored temporarily on our servers. Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

5. Third-Party Services

WebShield AI may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts for typography (subject to Google's privacy policy).
  • AI language model APIs for the Security Advisor feature. Your questions and scan context are sent to the AI provider to generate responses. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed password storage, and session-based authentication. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the WebShield AI team through the project repository or at the email provided in your account settings.
